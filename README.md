# 📊 HR Employee Attrition Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

**HR Employee Attrition Analytics** is a data analytics project focused on understanding employee turnover and identifying the key factors contributing to employee attrition.

The project analyzes employee-related factors such as **department, job role, overtime, business travel, distance from home, job satisfaction, environment satisfaction, work-life balance, years in the same role, and salary hikes** to provide actionable insights for HR teams and business leaders.

The dashboard helps organizations move from generic HR initiatives to **data-driven and targeted employee retention strategies**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A large organization with around **10,000 employees** was experiencing increasing employee turnover, affecting:

* Employee retention
* Project delivery
* Team stability
* Recruitment costs
* Training costs
* Organizational knowledge

The main challenge was the lack of clear visibility into **which employees, departments, and job roles were most affected by attrition and what factors were driving employee turnover**.

The reported overall attrition rate in the project is **22.47%**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The key objectives of this project are:

1. Calculate the overall employee attrition rate.
2. Identify departments with the highest attrition.
3. Analyze attrition across different job roles.
4. Understand the relationship between distance from home and attrition.
5. Analyze the impact of overtime on employee turnover.
6. Study the effect of job and environment satisfaction.
7. Analyze the relationship between work-life balance and attrition.
8. Understand whether frequent business travel contributes to employee turnover.
9. Analyze whether years in the same role influence attrition.
10. Evaluate whether salary hikes are effectively influencing employee retention.

---

## 📊 Key Areas of Analysis

### 👥 Employee Attrition

Analyze the overall employee turnover rate and understand the scale of the retention problem.

### 🏢 Department Analysis

Compare attrition levels across departments to identify high-risk areas.

### 💼 Job Role Analysis

Identify job roles experiencing higher employee turnover and determine where targeted retention strategies may be required.

### ✈️ Business Travel

Analyze whether employees who travel frequently for business are more likely to leave.

### ⏰ Overtime

Investigate whether employees working overtime have higher attrition rates.

### 🏠 Distance From Home

Study whether commuting distance is associated with increased employee turnover.

### 😊 Employee Satisfaction

Analyze how job satisfaction and environment satisfaction relate to employee retention.

### ⚖️ Work-Life Balance

Identify whether employees with lower work-life balance scores have a higher likelihood of leaving.

### 📈 Years in Current Role

Analyze whether staying in the same role for a longer period is associated with employee attrition.

### 💰 Salary Hikes

Evaluate whether salary increases are influencing employee retention effectively.
